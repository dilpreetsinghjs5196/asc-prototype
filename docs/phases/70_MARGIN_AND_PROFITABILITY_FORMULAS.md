## 1. MARGIN PHILOSOPHY & SEMANTIC BOUNDARIES

Ambulatory Surgery Centers operate under distinct layers of financial margin. Conflating **Contribution Margin** with **Net Profit** or **EBITDA** leads to catastrophic operational errors:
- **Contribution Margin** measures whether an incremental surgical case generates more revenue than the direct out-of-pocket cash required to perform it.
- **Operating Profitability** measures whether total facility contribution margins exceed fixed monthly building overhead and corporate administration.

ASCANALYST establishes mathematical formulas for 12 standardized margin metrics:

## 2. CANONICAL MARGIN FORMULAS

### Formula 1: Expected Contribution Margin
- **Canonical Code:** `expected_contribution_margin`
- **Scope:** Encounter / Case Level (Pre-Op & Post-Op Estimate)
- **Datatype:** `NUMERIC(12,2)`
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Expected Contribution Margin} = \text{Contract Total Allowable} - \text{Total Direct Case Cost}$$
- **Business Purpose:** The net dollars generated by a case toward covering fixed facility overhead.
- **Strict Rule:** Must NEVER be labeled as "Net Profit" or "EBITDA".

### Formula 2: Actual Contribution Margin
- **Canonical Code:** `actual_contribution_margin`
- **Scope:** Encounter / Case Level (Post-Adjudication Settled)
- **Datatype:** `NUMERIC(12,2)`
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Actual Contribution Margin} = \text{Total Net Collections} - \text{Total Direct Case Cost}$$
- **Business Purpose:** The true realized cash contribution margin after all insurance remittances and patient payments have cleared.

### Formula 3: Contribution Margin Percentage
- **Canonical Code:** `contribution_margin_pct`
- **Scope:** Case, Procedure, Surgeon, or Facility Aggregate
- **Datatype:** `NUMERIC(6,4)` (Rendered as Percentage)
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Contribution Margin Pct} = \frac{\text{Expected Contribution Margin}}{\text{Contract Total Allowable}} \times 100$$
- **Edge-Case Handling:**
  - If `Contract Total Allowable = 0`, return `0.00%` with status `INVALID_DENOMINATOR`.
  - If `Contract Total Allowable < 0`, flag error and suppress percentage display.
  - Negative margins (e.g. costs exceed allowable) must render clearly in Rose (`#f43f5e`) as a negative percentage.

### Formula 4: Contribution Margin Per OR Minute
- **Canonical Code:** `margin_per_or_minute`
- **Scope:** Room Efficiency / Scheduling Optimization
- **Datatype:** `NUMERIC(12,2)`
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Margin Per OR Minute} = \frac{\text{Expected Contribution Margin}}{\text{Surgical Duration Minutes (WheelsIn to WheelsOut)}}$$
- **Business Purpose:** Measures room throughput profitability. High-performing ASCs target $>\$45.00$ per OR minute.

### Formula 5: Contribution Margin Per OR Hour
- **Canonical Code:** `margin_per_or_hour`
- **Scope:** Block Schedule Optimization
- **Datatype:** `NUMERIC(12,2)`
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Margin Per OR Hour} = \text{Margin Per OR Minute} \times 60$$

### Formula 6: Procedure-Level Contribution Margin
- **Canonical Code:** `procedure_contribution_margin`
- **Scope:** CPT / HCPCS Procedural Evaluation
- **Datatype:** `NUMERIC(12,2)`
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Procedure Margin} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} \text{Case Margin for CPT}_k}{n_{\text{cases}}}$$
- **Business Purpose:** Identifies which surgical procedures are financially accretive vs. dilutive to facility operations.

### Formula 7: Surgeon-Level Contribution Margin
- **Canonical Code:** `surgeon_contribution_margin`
- **Scope:** Provider Performance & Credentialing
- **Datatype:** `NUMERIC(12,2)`
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Surgeon Contribution Margin} = \sum \text{Case Margins for Surgeon}_j$$
- **Surgeon Scorecard KPI:** Combines total margin, average margin per case, and margin per OR minute to rank surgeon block schedule requests.

### Formula 8: Payer-Level Contribution Margin
- **Canonical Code:** `payer_contribution_margin`
- **Scope:** Contract Negotiation & Managed Care
- **Datatype:** `NUMERIC(12,2)`
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Payer Margin} = \sum \text{Case Margins for Payer}_m$$
- **Business Purpose:** Evaluates commercial payer profitability during contract renewal negotiations.

### Formula 9: Facility Gross Profit (Accounting)
- **Canonical Code:** `facility_gross_profit`
- **Scope:** Facility Monthly Statement
- **Datatype:** `NUMERIC(12,2)`
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Gross Profit} = \text{Net Patient Service Revenue} - \text{Total Direct Clinical Expenses}$$

### Formula 10: Facility Operating Income (EBIT)
- **Canonical Code:** `facility_operating_income`
- **Scope:** Enterprise Income Statement
- **Datatype:** `NUMERIC(12,2)`
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Operating Income} = \text{Gross Profit} - \text{Fixed Overhead} - \text{SG\&A Expenses} - \text{Depreciation}$$

### Formula 11: Facility EBITDA
- **Canonical Code:** `facility_ebitda`
- **Scope:** Corporate Enterprise
- **Datatype:** `NUMERIC(12,2)`
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{EBITDA} = \text{Operating Income} + \text{Depreciation} + \text{Amortization}$$
- **Strict Rule:** EBITDA is an enterprise facility metric. It is mathematically invalid at the individual case level.

### Formula 12: Contractual Underpayment Leakage
- **Canonical Code:** `contractual_underpayment_leakage`
- **Scope:** Revenue Recovery
- **Datatype:** `NUMERIC(12,2)`
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Underpayment} = \text{Contract Total Allowable} - \text{Actual Payer Payment} - \text{Authorized Payer Adjustments}$$
- **Threshold Rule:** Variances exceeding $\$50.00$ are automatically routed to the Underpayment Appeal Queue.

## 3. EDGE-CASE & EXTREME VALUE BEHAVIOR

| Condition / Scenario | Formula Behavior | UI Presentation | Quality Status Flag |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Allowable = $0.00** | Division by zero trapped | Suppress %; display `$0.00` | `INVALID_DENOMINATOR` |
| **Negative Margin (Cost > Allowable)** | Compute negative dollar & % | Display in Rose (`text-rose-400`) | `DILUTIVE_CASE` |
| **Missing Implant Invoice** | Return `NULL` | Render "Incomplete" Badge | `MISSING_SOURCE_DATA` |
| **Pro Bono / Charity Case** | Allowable = $0.00; Direct Costs logged | Highlight as Pro Bono | `PRO_BONO_ENCOUNTER` |
| **Cosmetic Fixed Fee Case** | Allowable = Fixed Package Price | Margin computed against direct supplies | `COSMETIC_FIXED_FEE` |
